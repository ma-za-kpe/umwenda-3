# 3. Design direction

Status: Proposed as of this commit; it merges only once approved on its rendered phone and desktop views.

## Decision

**Umuryango Credit Circle**: Umwenda lets a Rwandan fintech analyst score a collateral-less trader on what the person can show, and let a trader see their standing without a parcel to pledge.

Tagline: Your standing, entered here, stands for the land.

## Why

The brand kit (`brand/BRAND.md`, `brand/tokens.css`, `brand/logo/`) sets the colours, type and voice every page uses, checked for contrast before any page is written.
