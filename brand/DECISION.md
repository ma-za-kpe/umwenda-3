# Brand decision: Umuryango Credit Circle

Status: accepted by the brand strategist; the design evaluator judges it on the built product.

## Decision

Umuryango Credit Circle, because the cooperative-ledger and canopy identity is native to the Rwandan context of communal standing rather than a parcel of land, and the deep-green-with-gold palette is committed enough to be remembered.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Banker's Slate — cool trust-blue fintech with a white-on-sky**: Every lender in the category already wears blue; the evaluator would not name this product in a hallway, and it says nothing about why this one stands out from EMMA.
- **Ledger Black &amp; Cream — high-contrast monochrome paperwork**: It reads as a tax form, not a credit circle; the traders it is for would not sit at a desk that looks like their creditor's.
- **Market-Bright Green — bright leaf-green fintech**: Too light and too 'insurance-adjacent'; the canopy is deep, not lime, and the brightness would fight the quiet the circle needs.

## Risks

- The deep green can read as insurance or agriculture if the gold accent is overused
- 'Credit circle' is a loaded term for a rejected study, so copy must never promise approval

## Reversal

Revisit this decision when: If the evaluator flags the green as too close to an earlier crew product's palette, keep the deep-green ground and shift the accent to the canopy's warm amber.
